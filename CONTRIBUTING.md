# Contributing to KumaDrone

Read the [KumaDrone Hobby License](LICENSE.md) and [contributor guide](AGENTS.md) before submitting changes. For design changes, include the editable source and a description of what changed. Report the checks you ran and any outstanding fit or bench work.

You keep ownership of your contribution. Before a pull request can be merged, the maintainers need your explicit agreement to the grant below so the project owner can release the contribution under the hobby license and use it in commercial KumaDrone products. An ordinary pull request or sign-off alone is insufficient for that separate grant.

Include this statement in your pull request if you agree:

> I have the right to submit this contribution and grant these permissions. For the material I submit in this pull request, I grant the owner of the original KumaDrone design a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, and sublicense the contribution, including for commercial KumaDrone products and releases. The project owner may exercise those rights directly or through its designated business. I agree that the project may also distribute my contribution to the public under the KumaDrone Hobby License 1.0.

Each rights holder needs to give assent. Flag any employer-owned work, jointly authored files, third-party material, or patent rights that may require separate permission before a merge. Provide provenance for any third-party content and retain its existing notices.
